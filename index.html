About

Michael started sim racing in rFactor on a low-end laptop with a gamepad, and in 2015 he won his first championship that way. He later reached a 2.3k iRating on iRacing's old road licence using a Logitech F310, and in 2024 he won two Pro-class championships on a controller. He has raced on a Moza R9 direct-drive wheel since then.

He races for Legion of Racers Ascent and coaches other drivers. His focus is competitive esports. In 2026 he raced the Porsche and Ferrari Esports Asia-Pacific qualifiers, finished second in the Sporting Class of the TGR Philippines eSports GT Championship, and was chosen with two other drivers to represent the Philippines at the TGR Asia eSports GT Championship in Bangkok in November. He also represents the Philippines in both the Assetto Corsa and iRacing categories at the Asia Pacific Motorsport Championship in Pattaya in October.

2026 season

  • TGR Philippines eSports GT Championship

    At the Gran Turismo 7 national finals in Pasay on 27 September he won Race 2 on dirt at Sardegna and finished second in the Sporting Class, the third highest score across the Sporting and Junior classes. That made him one of the three drivers chosen to represent the Philippines at the TGR Asia eSports GT Championship in Bangkok on 14 and 15 November.

  • Asia Pacific Motorsport Championship

    He represents the Philippines in both esports categories, Assetto Corsa and iRacing, at the 2026 championship at Bira Circuit in Pattaya, Thailand, on 23 to 25 October, as the only esports driver in an 18-driver Philippine team. Teams enter through their national motorsport bodies, and the Philippine team won the overall title at the 2025 edition.

  • Porsche Esports Supercup, Asia-Pacific

    He raced the regional qualifiers and finals in the Porsche 911 GT3 Cup, and the races were shown live on the official iRacing broadcast.

  • Ferrari Esports, Asia-Pacific

    He raced the Asia-Pacific regional qualifier.

  • AAP National Esports Championship

    He won the first Circuit round in the Pro class, racing in Le Mans Ultimate.

  • eRacingGP Endurance Edition

    He finished second in the Pro class in a Ferrari 296 GT3, racing in Assetto Corsa Competizione.

Coaching

Michael coaches drivers in one-on-one sessions on Discord. He goes through your laps with you corner by corner to find where the time is going, then explains what to change in your driving and in the car’s setup.

How a session works

  1. Before the sessionYou tell him the sim, the car, the track and your best lap time.
  2. On DiscordHe watches your laps with you and goes through them corner by corner.
  3. After the sessionYou leave with the changes to make in your driving and in the car’s setup.

Who it’s for

Drivers on a wheel or a controller who want to find time, whether they race in leagues or are getting ready for a competition.
